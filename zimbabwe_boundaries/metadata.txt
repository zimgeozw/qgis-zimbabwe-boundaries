about=Gives you Zimbabwe's official administrative boundaries in one click, with no internet connection needed: the country outline, the 10 provinces, the 91 districts and the 1,961 wards of 2018 (the old ward boundaries, before the latest delimitation). Each layer carries the official P-codes and the names of the units above it. Filter by province and district, choose a coordinate system (WGS 84, UTM 35S, UTM 36S or any other), and add the layers to the map or save them as Shapefile, GeoPackage, GeoJSON or KML, optionally zipped. Data: Zimbabwe National Statistics Agency (ZIMSTAT) and OCHA ROSEA, Common Operational Dataset of administrative boundaries (COD-AB), valid from 11 September 2018, from the Humanitarian Data Exchange, licensed CC BY-IGO.
